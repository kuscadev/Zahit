# About Zahit

Zahit is more than just a theme; it is a perspective. It represents a way of highlighting what truly matters by stripping away the unnecessary. Zahit is a symbol of doing more with less, of finding depth within simplicity in the digital space.

## Features

- Clean, minimalist design
- Full dark mode support
- Responsive layout for all devices
- Easy content management with Astro components
- Markdown and MDX support
- External blog posts support (Medium, Substack, etc.)
- Code syntax highlighting for developers
- Tags and categorization
- SEO‑friendly structure
- High performance with minimal JavaScript
- Smooth transitions and subtle animations

## Philosophy

Zahit sees simplicity not as a lack, but as a strength. To leave behind what is unnecessary and bring forward what is meaningful… This theme offers creators a quiet space: distraction‑free, simple yet profound. Zahit is a pause in the digital world, a place where readers meet the text and writers meet their essence.

## Technology Stack

Built with [Astro](https://astro.build), Zahit avoids heavy frameworks to remain lightweight and flexible. Styled with handcrafted CSS, it provides a solid foundation for your content while keeping speed and clarity at the core.
